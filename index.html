Research philosophy

Evidence first. Judgement second.

The purpose of each project is not to produce a convincing narrative. It is to make a useful analytical claim, test competing explanations and show where the evidence remains uncertain.

01

Frame the question

Define the decision, scope and what a credible answer would require.

02

Trace the evidence

Prioritise primary sources, transparent provenance and reproducible data.

03

Challenge the thesis

Test alternative explanations, scenarios and disconfirming information.

04

State the judgement

Communicate conclusions, uncertainty, limitations and implications clearly.

About

My work draws on academic training in political economy and security studies, professional exposure to geopolitical monitoring at M&C Saatchi World Services, and independent study of financial markets and quantitative analysis.
